Revenue Core — what you take from Revenue Cloud, and what you build instead

The blueprint assumed Revenue Cloud Advanced (RCA) is enabled. Your org is a Developer Edition: no RCA, no Experience Cloud, no sandboxes, 5 MB data storage, 2 licences. This folder maps every RCA dependency and the replacement you build yourself — Revenue Core.

Two corrections to earlier advice

1. Do NOT build Invoice__c / Invoice_Line__c. DATA_MODEL.md §18 forbids pre-building them, and E10 is conditional on Q3 / BQ7. Until Finance answers, those use cases must not be started.
2. ActivationService and the subscription state machine are not new work — they are already UC-COM-03 and UC-COM-04. The genuinely new work created by the missing licence is the pricing engine, the configuration rules, and the classification hierarchy.

Permanently blocked

  • E7 — Self-Service Portal needs an Experience Cloud licence. No workaround gives the real thing; only the public unauthenticated form survives (UC-ACQ-03).
  • Scratch orgs / sandboxes — a DE org is not a Dev Hub. All testing happens in the dev org, with careful reset scripts.
  • “5 GB of prices” in E2's exit gate cannot be tested literally — test the pattern at small scale, document the scaling strategy (file 02 §3).
Non-negotiable rule: scope stays frozen at the 80 use cases. Revenue Core is not a generic pricing engine — no tiers, slabs, rate matrices, usage rating, multi-currency or proration.

“Close or explicitly defer each decision, recording the decision, date, and rationale.” With no sponsor meetings available, the pragmatic move is:

1 · Answer yourself now
N1, N2, N4, N5, N7, N8 — they are architectural, and you are the architect.
2 · Answer by circumstance
Q2 and Q3/BQ7 — no SKU exists on a DE org. Record as deferred-with-rationale.
3 · Carry as assumptions
Q1/BQ1, Q4/BQ8, BQ2–BQ12 — placeholder values tagged ASSUMPTION — pending <owner>.
None of them block the Revenue Core build. Every assumption you write becomes a one-line change later — a validation-rule value, a formula, an Engagement_Penalty__mdt row.
