Transitions enforced by SubscriptionTriggerHandler. Cancelled and Superseded are terminal.

Transition Rules

  • Draft → PendingActivation: Initial activation or after cancellation reversal
  • PendingActivation → Active: BSS provisioning successful
  • PendingActivation → PendingCancellation: Cancellation request submitted
  • Active → Suspended: Temporarily suspend service
  • Active → PendingCancellation: Cancellation submitted while active
  • Active → Superseded: Replaced by a new subscription (plan change/upgrade)
  • Suspended → Active: Resume service
  • Suspended → PendingCancellation: Cancel during suspension
  • PendingCancellation → Cancelled: Termination completed
  • Superseded → (replacement points here): New subscription takes over
  • Cancelled and Superseded: Terminal — never hard-delete

Status is the only mutable lifecycle field on Subscription__c. No automation writes it except the trigger.
